Frequently asked questions

Will it damage my door or paint?

It attaches with double-sided adhesive rather than screws. Clean the surface first, and peel it off slowly when you want to remove it.

How is it powered?

A built-in 300 mAh lithium-ion battery, charged through the Type-C port. A small light shows charging status.

How long is the cord?

The elastic cord is about 1.8 m, and the toy lifts to random heights within that length.

Is it suitable for kittens and senior cats?

Yes, most cats enjoy it. Watch how your cat plays at first, and pick a mounting height that suits their jumping ability.

Can I leave it running while I'm out?

We recommend supervised play. Switch it off and put the cord out of reach when you're done.

Which colors are available?

Pink and blue. Both include the same rainbow caterpillar teaser with a feather tail and bell.
